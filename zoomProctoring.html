Online Exam Instructions

The goal of this page is to help orient you to our testing expectation.

For this to work, you will need to find a quiet place to take your exam. Depending upon your living situtation, this may be difficult in your dorm room. Please make every attempt to find a safe place to take the exam.

NOTE: Students with either Disability Resource (DR) accommodations or a make-up exam time will take their exam in person in the DR office. Arrangements must be made in advance, therefore it is important to know and follow the DR policies.

Device Requirements

For proctoring these exams, you will need:
  • an exam device (e.g. laptop) for accessing the exam
  • a proctor device (e.g. cell phone) for proctoring (See below for how the proctor device should be positioned)

Exam Device

On this device only , you will access the exam via Webassign (with the link provided on Canvas). This exam will be password protected with the password provided once the exam begins.

Proctor Device

On this device only , you will log into Zoom and turn your camera on. There will be a specific Zoom link provided by your instructor.

Exam Session

You must join the exam Zoom link at least 10 minutes before the exam. This is important so that any technical issues can be ironed out before the exaam begins. Please do check in on your proctor device to see if we have sent you any messages indicating your set up is not sufficient. Video must be enabled on your proctor device. You won't be able to see anyone else's video due to a specific setting in Zoom (called focus mode).

Exam Submission

There is no overall 'submit' button for Webassign. Rather, you submit each individual question. Do not wait until the end of the exam to submit your questions! Submit them as you go. There is no limit to the number of submissions. Every exam some students lose points unnecessarily due to having unsubmitted solutions

For the mid-semester exams (Exam 1 and Exam 2), you must stay signed into Zoom until the exam ends at 7:50 pm, even if you finish early. You can work on other class work as long as it is on paper or involves a physical book. You cannot use your exam device or proctor device until the exam is over.

Internet or Browser Issues

An exam is valid only if it is proctored. If your device drops the Zoom proctor session, you must reconnect to the Zoom proctor session as soon as possible. Once you reconnect,immediately inform the proctor of what happened, using chat on you proctor device. If too much time elapses, a make up exam may be required.

If you experience a brief drop in connection we will flag your exam for inspection, but we will not automatically invalidate it. We reserve the right to conduct “spot check” interviews after the exam to ask selected students to explain (not reproduce from scratch) their answer or approach to one or a few exam questions to the instructor.
