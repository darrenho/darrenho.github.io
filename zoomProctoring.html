Online Exam Instructions

The goal of this page is to help orient you to our testing expectation.

For this to work, you will need to find a quiet place to take your exam. Depending upon your living situtation, this may be difficult in your dorm room. Please make every attempt to find a suitable place to take the exam.

NOTE: Students with Disability Resource (DR) accommodations will take their exam via this proctoring method and not through DR. Your DR accomodation will be incorporated into your exam.

Device Requirements

For proctoring these exams, you will need:
  • an exam device (e.g. laptop) for accessing the exam. Do not log into Zoom on this device!
  • a proctor device (e.g. cell phone) for proctoring (See below for how the proctor device should be positioned). Note that an external webcam with suitably long cord would suffice.

Exam Device

On this device, you will access the exam via Webassign (with the link provided on Canvas). This exam will be password protected with the password provided once the exam begins. To emphasize: do not log into Zoom on this device. If your exam device has multiple screens they must all be equally visable via the proctor device.

Proctor Device

On this device only, you will log into Zoom and turn your camera on and sound off. There will be a specific Zoom link provided by your instructor. Do not ask if your exam set up is good. If you follow the examples here it will be fine. We will contact you if it is not sufficient.

Please do check in on your proctor device to see if we have sent you any messages indicating your set up is not sufficient. Video must be enabled on your proctor device. You won't be able to see anyone else's video due to a specific setting in Zoom (called focus mode).

Exam Session

You must join the exam Zoom link at least 10 minutes before the exam. This is important so that any technical issues can be ironed out before the exam begins. At some point during this time period, hold your student id up to your proctor device camera next to your face for identification purposes. No feedback will follow but it will be a part of the exam recording.

Allowed Materials During Exam

For the exams, you are allowed to use:
  • R/Rstudio as well as R scripts containing code and code comments (VS Code is acceptable)
  • Webassign for accessing the exam
  • Physical calculator (e.g. from Texas Instruments)
  • Desmos for integration and analytic geometry
  • 1 @ 8.5" by 11" piece of paper both sides. You can put anything you want in this piece of paper
    • (3 pages will be allowed on the final)
  • Scratch paper. This must be blank at the start of the exam
  • Distribution applet. A link to this will be included when applicable on homework/exam questions

Exam Submission

There is no overall 'submit' button for Webassign. Rather, you submit each individual question. Do not wait until the end of the exam to submit your questions! Submit them as you go. There is no limit to the number of submissions. Every exam some students lose points unnecessarily due to having unsubmitted solutions

You must stay signed into Zoom until the exam ends, even if you finish early. You can work on other class work as long as it is on paper or involves a physical book. You cannot use your exam device or proctor device until the exam is over.

Internet or Browser Issues

An exam is valid only if it is proctored. If your proctor device drops out of Zoom, you must reconnect as soon as possible. Once you reconnect,immediately inform the proctor of what happened, using chat on you proctor device. If too much time elapses, a make up exam may be required.

If you experience a brief drop in connection we will flag your exam for inspection, but we will not automatically invalidate it. We reserve the right to conduct “spot check” interviews after the exam to ask selected students to explain (not reproduce from scratch) their answer or approach to one or a few exam questions to the instructor.
